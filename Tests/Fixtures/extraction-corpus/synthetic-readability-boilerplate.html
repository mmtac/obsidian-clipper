Attackers Exploit New Linux Flaw

The vulnerability, tracked under the name *sshole, arises from the interaction between two common packages, and researchers say machines running both can be taken over in a matter of seconds without any password at all.

A security firm said on Monday that honeypots it maintains had come under attack by a group conducting what it called mature intrusions, and that it had gained access to one of the attackers' servers and recovered several of their tools.

The group is also installing extra software on machines that lack it, because the backdoor it uses depends on that software to run, which gives defenders another signal to look for when hunting for compromised systems.

Maintainers have released a fix and urge administrators to apply it immediately, while noting that systems which do not combine the two packages are not affected and need no further action beyond the usual updates.

Researchers expect exploitation to continue for weeks, since many embedded devices are rarely patched, and say the episode is a reminder that a bug in a small library can become a large problem very quickly.
