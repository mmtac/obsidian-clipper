Why Plain Text Notes Outlast Every App

By Sam Rivera · 6 min read

Every few years a new note-taking app promises to organize your life. Every few years, people export their notes, lose half the formatting, and start again. Plain text breaks that cycle.

Files you actually own

A Markdown file is just text. It opens in any editor, on any device, decades from now. Links, headings, and lists survive every migration because there is nothing proprietary to lose.

The best archive is the one you can still read without the software that made it.

Capture first, organize later

  • Save articles the moment you find them.
  • Keep the source link and date with every note.
  • Let search and backlinks do the filing.

Over time, a folder of small, linked notes becomes something more useful than any single app: a personal knowledge base that grows with you.
