### Description (≤4000)
Markdown Clipper saves what you read into your Obsidian vault as clean, portable Markdown — right from the iOS Share Sheet.

Tap Share in Safari or any other app, choose MD Clipper, and the page lands in your vault as its own folder: a Markdown note with YAML frontmatter, plus an images folder with every picture from the article. No Obsidian plugin is required — the app writes plain .md files straight into the folder you pick in Files, on iCloud Drive or on your device.

WHAT IT DOES
• Article extraction — strips menus, ads, cookie banners, and related links, keeping just the story.
• Images included — downloads article images next to the note and links them inline.
• On-device OCR — recognizes text inside images with Apple Vision and adds it to the note, so it's searchable.
• Works from any app — Safari, Mail, Messages, news and social apps, Photos. Links shared as text are detected automatically.
• Safari captures what you see — including pages you're signed in to.
• Site Logins (optional) — sign in once to sites you subscribe to, so links shared from other apps save the full article.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Everything runs on your device. No servers, no accounts, no analytics.
• Notes go only to the folder you choose.
• Site sign-ins are stored in your device's Keychain and sent only to that site.

OBSIDIAN-FRIENDLY OUTPUT
• Frontmatter with title, source URL, and clip date — ready for Dataview.
• Self-contained clip folders: move a note and its images move with it.
• Headings, lists, code blocks, quotes, tables, and links preserved.

Markdown Clipper for Obsidian is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Obsidian or Dynalist Inc.
